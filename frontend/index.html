Industry Challenge

The Cost of Reactive Infrastructure Maintenance

Power utilities discover catastrophic failures only after a substation breaker trips or a transformer catches fire.

Unplanned Blackouts

Sudden dielectric breakdown causes multi-hour industrial grid outages and millions in penalty costs.

Unprioritized Work Orders

Maintenance teams dispatch technicians to healthy units while critical degraded units near thermal flashover remain uninspected.

Black-Box AI Skepticism

Engineers reject opaque probability numbers without clear, physics-informed contributing factors explaining why an asset is at risk.

Core Architecture

Engineered for Industrial Decision Support

Explainable Risk Scoring

Deconstructs composite risk into transparent additive factors: winding temperature surges, harmonic vibration, load stress, and maintenance interval gaps.

Machine Learning Prediction

Calibrated failure probability modeling (0–100%) trained on multi-variable transformer degradation features with interpretable coefficient analysis.

Resource Prioritization

Algorithmic dispatch index ranking transformers across substations to optimize constrained maintenance crews and minimize system downtime.

What-If Scenario Simulator

Simulate hypothetical grid stress scenarios (+15% load, +5°C temperature) to preview risk score deltas before implementing feeder reconfigurations.

7-Stage Action Tracker

Complete end-to-end task progression: Detected → Risk Assessed → Inspection Required → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Verified.

Field Incident Escalation

Technician observations immediately update asset risk models and trigger automated high-severity alerts when abnormal physical conditions recur.
